# Order Tracking

## Tracking code
We email a tracking code as soon as your order is dispatched by the carrier. You can also find it in your account under Orders. Orders that are still processing have no tracking code yet.

## Order status meanings
Processing means we are preparing your package. In transit means the carrier has it and is delivering it. Delivered means the carrier confirmed the delivery. Canceled means the order will not be shipped.

## Carriers
We ship with FastShip, SwiftPost and Loggi. Use the tracking code on the carrier website for detailed location updates.

## Tracking not updating
Tracking events can take up to 24 hours to appear after dispatch. If the status has not changed for more than 3 business days, contact support with your order number.
