# Payments

## Accepted payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal and store gift cards. Installment plans are not available. Cash and bank transfers are not accepted.

## When you are charged
Your card is authorized at checkout and charged when the order ships. If an item is out of stock, the authorization is released and you are not charged.

## Declined payments
A payment can be declined because of insufficient funds, a wrong billing address or a card security block. Check the details or try another method. Contact your card issuer if the problem continues.

## Invoices and security
An invoice is emailed after the order ships. We never ask for your full card number, security code or password by email or chat.
