# Privacy and Data

## What we collect
We collect only the data needed to process orders: name, email, delivery address and payment confirmation. We never store your full card number.

## Who sees your data
Support agents see only the last four digits of a card. We never sell or share your personal data with advertisers. Carriers receive only the name and address needed for delivery.

## Your data rights
You can request an export or the deletion of your personal data at any time through support. We complete the request within 30 days. Deleting an account does not remove invoices that the law requires us to keep.

## Sensitive information in conversations
Do not type ID numbers or card numbers in chat. Our assistant automatically hides them from tickets and logs.
