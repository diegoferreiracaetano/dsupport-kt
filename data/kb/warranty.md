# Warranty

## Coverage
Products have a 12 month warranty against manufacturing defects, counted from the delivery date. Accessories such as cables and chargers have a 6 month warranty.

## What is not covered
The warranty does not cover accidental damage, liquid damage, normal wear, unauthorized repairs or misuse.

## How to make a warranty claim
Contact support with your order number, a description of the problem and photos or a video of the defect. We review the claim within 3 business days.

## Repair or replacement
If the claim is approved we repair the product or send a replacement. When neither is possible we refund the amount paid. Warranty shipping costs are paid by us.
