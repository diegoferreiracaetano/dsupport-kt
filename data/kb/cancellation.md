# Order Cancellation

## Canceling before shipping
You can cancel for free while the order status is processing. Open Orders in your account, choose the order and select Cancel order. The refund is issued within 3 to 5 business days.

## Canceling after shipping
After the order is dispatched it can no longer be canceled. Refuse the delivery or return the package once it arrives, following the return policy.

## Personalized items
Personalized items cannot be canceled after production starts, which usually happens within 24 hours of the purchase.

## Canceled by us
We cancel an order automatically if payment fails or if the item is out of stock. You are never charged for an order we cancel.
