# Returns Policy

## Return window
You can return most items within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging and include all accessories. The 30 days count from the delivery date shown in your tracking details.

## How to start a return
Sign in to your account, open Orders, choose the order and select Return item. Pick the reason and we email you a prepaid return label. Ship the package within 7 days after the label is issued.

## Return shipping costs
Return shipping is free when the item is defective, damaged or not what you ordered. For any other reason the return shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

## Items that cannot be returned
Gift cards, personalized items, perishable goods and opened software cannot be returned. Items returned after the 30 day window are not accepted.
