# Shipping

## Delivery speeds
Standard shipping takes 5 to 7 business days. Express shipping takes 2 business days. Orders placed before 2 p.m. on a business day ship the same day.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free for orders of $50 or more. Below that amount standard shipping costs $4.99. Express shipping costs $12.99 on every order.

## Where we ship
We ship to the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom and the European Union. We do not ship to PO boxes with express delivery. Customers are responsible for customs duties and import taxes on international orders.

## Changing the address
You can change the delivery address while the order is still processing. After the order is dispatched the address cannot be changed.
