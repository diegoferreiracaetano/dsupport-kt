# Your Account

## Resetting your password
Select Forgot password on the sign in page and enter your email. We send a reset link that expires after 1 hour. If the email does not arrive, check your spam folder.

## Changing your details
You can change your name, email and delivery addresses in Account settings. Changing the email requires confirming the new address with a code.

## Two factor authentication
Two factor authentication is optional and adds a code to each sign in. Turn it on in Account settings under Security.

## Locked accounts
After 5 failed sign in attempts your account is locked for 30 minutes. Reset your password to unlock it sooner.
