# Refunds

## When you get your money back
A refund is issued after we receive and inspect the returned item. The refund takes 5 to 7 business days to be processed. Credit card issuers can take another 2 to 3 business days to show the amount on your statement.

## Where the refund goes
Refunds always go back to the original payment method. We cannot refund to a different card or account. Gift card purchases are refunded as store credit.

## Shipping fees
Original shipping fees are refunded only when the item was defective or wrong, or when the order was canceled before it shipped. In every other case the shipping fee is not refundable.

## Partial refunds
If an item comes back used, damaged or without its accessories, we may issue a partial refund. We email you the reason and the amount before processing it.
