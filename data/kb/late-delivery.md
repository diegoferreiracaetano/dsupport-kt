# Late Delivery

## Estimated delivery date
Every order shows an estimated delivery date. Delays can happen because of weather, holidays or carrier capacity. A package is late when it is still in transit after the estimated date.

## What to do when your package is late
Wait up to 3 business days after the estimated date. If the package is still not delivered, contact support with your order number and the email used in the purchase. We open a ticket and contact the carrier within 1 business day.

## Lost packages
If a package does not arrive within 15 days after the estimated date we consider it lost. We then send a replacement or issue a full refund, including the shipping fee, as you prefer.

## Compensation
When a delay is our responsibility, the shipping fee of the late order is refunded. Support decides each case after checking with the carrier.
